# Privacy Policy for Swipe2Merge

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**

Swipe2Merge is a mobile puzzle game provided by Crane Royal Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Swipe2Merge stores game progress, scores, selected difficulty, language, theme, sound settings, and music settings locally on your device.

Crane Royal Studios does not receive this information. You can delete it by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

## 2. Personal information

Swipe2Merge does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photographs, precise location, or other directly identifying personal information.

## 3. Advertising

Advertising is disabled in the current release of Swipe2Merge, and the application does not request or display advertisements.

The application includes an optional integration with the Google Mobile Ads SDK for a possible future release. If advertising is enabled in the future, this Privacy Policy and the application's Google Play Data safety information will be updated before publication.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. In-app purchases

In-app purchases are disabled in the current release.

If purchases are enabled in the future, payments will be processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Crane Royal Studios will not directly receive or store payment-card details.

## 5. Children

Swipe2Merge is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under the applicable age of digital consent.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user clears the application's data or uninstalls the application.

Because Crane Royal Studios does not receive this local information, deletion requests are not necessary. Users can remove it directly from their device.

## 7. Security

Swipe2Merge does not transmit locally stored game progress or settings to Crane Royal Studios.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the application is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of user information.

## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The current version will remain available at this URL, and the effective date at the top of the policy will be updated.
